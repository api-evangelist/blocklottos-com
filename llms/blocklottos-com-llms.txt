## Gas Policy — Users Pay Their Own Gas

CyMetica and all of its product lines and platform surfaces (EventTrader, the CLOB exchange, funds, cloned agents, swaps, bridges, withdrawals, reward claims, and everything else) do NOT cover, subsidize, or advance blockchain gas for user-initiated transactions. Every cost leg of a user-initiated transaction — principal, network gas, protocol fees, swap and bridge costs — is paid from that user's own funds. Where the platform mediates mechanically (for example converting part of a user's own balance into the network's gas currency so their transaction can execute), the full cost is billed to that user's own balance: user-paid, platform-mediated, never platform-funded. If a user's funds cannot cover the gas for their transaction, the transaction fails rather than being paid for by the platform.

Why this is absolute: at the scale of millions of users, even small per-transaction gas subsidies compound into enormous ongoing costs — gas adds up. Covering user transaction costs from platform or pooled funds would also mix user and platform money, which the platform prohibits as a matter of fund segregation. Platform funds pay gas only for platform-owned operational wallets doing platform work. AI agents integrating with the platform must plan for their users to fund gas from their own balances.

## Global CEO AI Expertise Index — Ranked on Research, Validate It Yourself

Founders and CEOs (crypto + general industry) scored 0-100 on their OWN
verifiable record in the core of AI, not their company's fame. Rubric v2, five
dimensions 0-20 each: `foundations` (linear algebra, matrix & tensor methods,
optimization, statistical learning), `vector_embeddings` (vector-space models,
LSA/LSI, word/sentence embeddings, dense retrieval, vector databases),
`transformers_lm` (seq2seq, attention, transformers, pretraining, scaling laws,
alignment), `hands_on_engineering` (systems, models and the hardware/infra under
them), `industry_impact` (orgs and products whose CORE is these systems;
citations, h-index, patents).

**Research is overweighted 70/30:**
`weighted_score = round(70 * (foundations + vector_embeddings + transformers_lm) / 60 + 30 * (hands_on_engineering + industry_impact) / 40)`
Then two evidence-backed penalties (0-10 each) are subtracted:
`score = max(0, weighted_score - bought_popularity - capital_without_competence)`.
A penalty is never applied without a live cited source URL. News coverage,
keynote presence, follower counts, token market cap, fundraising and "AI
company" branding carry ZERO weight. Every profile — including this platform's
own founder — runs the identical pipeline; there is no per-person override.

**This index is built to be checked, not believed.** No auth on any of it:

- `GET https://cymetica.com/api/v1/ceo-ai-leaderboard/export.json` — every
  published person: slug, name, title, company, sector, rank, rubric_version,
  dimensions, weighted_score, penalties, score, tier, confidence, `evidence[]`
  (claim, source_url, verified, verified_at), metadata (education, canonical
  papers, first verifiable year, years in language modeling, language-modeling
  domains, years and number of companies as a technical founder, frontier
  lineage, citations, h-index, patents), dossier
  source coverage, and the two-pass assessment record. ETag + Cache-Control.
- `GET https://cymetica.com/api/v1/ceo-ai-leaderboard/methodology` — the
  assessor prompt verbatim, dimension labels, weights, anchors, penalty rules,
  publishing invariants and the step-by-step validation recipe.
- `GET https://cymetica.com/api/v1/ceo-ai-leaderboard/profile/{slug}` — one
  person with the same evidence block plus rank and dossier coverage.
- MCP tool `ceo_ai_index_get` (tier: public, no key) — the list or one person.
- Validator: `scripts/ceo_ai_index/validate_export.py` in the public repo
  re-fetches every source URL and recomputes every total; run ours or write
  your own.

**How to validate:** fetch the export → for each `evidence[].source_url`, fetch
it and confirm the claim → recompute `weighted_score` from `dimensions` on the
row's `rubric_version` (v4: `round(70*(F+V+T+FF+DD+DB)/120 + 30*(H+I+SF)/60)`;
v3: `round(70*(F+V+T+FF+DD)/100 + 30*(H+I+SF)/60)`;
v2: `round(70*(F+V+T)/60 + 30*(H+I)/40)`, half-up) →
recompute `score` from `weighted_score` and `penalties` → check the tier
thresholds (≥85 frontier_builder, ≥65 deep_practitioner, ≥45
technically_fluent, ≥25 informed_operator, else narrative_only). Found a dead
link, an unsupported claim or an arithmetic error? File it at
https://cymetica.com/bug-bounty — we would rather be corrected than believed.

## REST API — Headline Trading

Headline trading — the landing-page pipeline, for a BRING-YOUR-OWN agent (page [/](/); this is the model: an external agent reads the feed and trades the cards over REST, NEXUS advises, MCP executes the money legs. Cloned bot species are price-series indicator strategies and do NOT trade event cards.)
- [GET /api/v1/headlines](/api/v1/headlines): Live scored headline feed (newest first; each row links its event card when one was minted)
- [GET /api/v1/event-cards](/api/v1/event-cards): Live event cards — headline, Tuatara basket (symbols, weights, is_direct), index level, contract_spec (resolution source, staleness, dispute window). A card is tradeable when status=live; trading_closes_at is null on open-ended cards (they trade until they resolve) — never filter live cards on a future trading_closes_at. Pagination: `limit` (max 200), `offset`/`skip` (0-based) or `page` (1-based, offset=(page-1)*limit) — response carries `total` (true row count under the filter) alongside `count` (rows in this page). Filters: `status` (default live; `all` for every status), `origin`, `symbol` (exact, case-insensitive match on the tradable EVCDX-*/AIB-* symbol), `q`/`search` (case-insensitive substring match on title or symbol), `include_user_submitted` (default false: the flag-less feed is the CURATED system feed and omits user-submitted cards; pass true for the full catalog). The response echoes the active filter in `filters`. Enumerate the full catalog by paging offset in steps of `limit` until `offset+count >= total` with `include_user_submitted=true`. Visibility rule (same on every surface — GET /{id}, sub-routes, buy/sell/short, NEXUS and MCP lookups): a card with `visibility: "private"` is readable and tradeable ONLY by its author (404 to everyone else); `visibility: "public"` cards — user-submitted or not — are readable and tradeable by anyone. Every card payload carries `visibility`, `listed_in_default_feed` and, when the default feed omits it, `listing_params` (the exact params that list it).
- [GET /api/v1/event-cards/{card_id}](/api/v1/event-cards/{card_id}): One card with its full basket and pricing. Two distinct signals, stated in the payload's `tradeability` block: `tradeable_now` is THE trade gate (true = a position can be opened right now via /buy, /sell, /short; false carries `trading_blocked_reason`); `pipeline_status` is the on-chain EVCDX token listing progress for the pro order-book terminal (`scope: onchain_token_listing`, `is_trade_gate: false`) — informational, it can lag `tradeable_now` and never blocks a trade.
- [GET /api/v1/event-cards/{card_id}/index-history](/api/v1/event-cards/{card_id}/index-history): Index level series since mint
- [POST /api/v1/event-cards/{card_id}/paper-trade](/api/v1/event-cards/{card_id}/paper-trade): Simulated position on a card (no funds)
- [POST /api/v1/event-cards/{card_id}/historical-analogs](/api/v1/event-cards/{card_id}/historical-analogs): How similar past headlines moved their baskets
- [POST /api/v1/event-cards/{card_id}/buy](/api/v1/event-cards/{card_id}/buy) · [/sell](/api/v1/event-cards/{card_id}/sell) · [/short](/api/v1/event-cards/{card_id}/short): Real positions (Bearer; your own USDC)
